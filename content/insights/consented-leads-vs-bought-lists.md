Every sales team has been offered the shortcut: a list of names and numbers, sorted by area or income band, ready to call.

It looks efficient. In practice it's often the most expensive lead you can buy. Most people on the list never asked to hear from you, don't remember how you got their number, and aren't ready to talk. Your team spends its day on calls that go nowhere, and your brand is the one they hang up on.

There's a better model: leads who chose to engage, agreed to be contacted and arrived with enough signal to prioritise.

## Why bought lists underperform

**They're cold.** A name on a list tells you nothing about intent. Your team has to create interest from nothing, call after call.

**They're unscored.** Every lead looks the same, so the warm ones get the same treatment as the cold ones, and often get it too late.

**They carry risk.** South Africa's data protection rules, including POPIA, place real limits on direct marketing to people who haven't consented. A list of unknown origin makes it hard to show where consent came from.

## What a consented lead looks like

A good lead has done three things:

1. **Engaged.** They took an action: answered questions, asked for information or started a conversation.
2. **Consented.** They agreed to be contacted, clearly and on the record.
3. **Signalled.** What they told you helps your team decide who to call first.

## How we built it for Leadforms

Leadforms (leadforms.co.za) is a survey-led lead-generation platform we built around that model.

- **A survey portal people want to use.** Short, themed surveys with a prize draw ("Stand a chance to win…") across 10 verticals, from life and funeral cover to property and trading. Each one carries the right risk and consent notices.
- **Consent captured on the web.** Sandy, a WhatsApp agent, engages respondents and sends them survey links, so completion and consent happen on the web, where they're captured cleanly.
- **A warmth score on every lead.** A four-signal scoring model means each lead arrives with a score, not just a name and number.
- **A legal stack built around consent.** Terms, a POPIA notice and a privacy policy designed around a revised consent model.
- **Automation behind it.** Voice-agent outreach and automated workflows feed the surveys.

The platform secured a proof of concept, in principle, with the mass-market distribution channel of a major South African life insurer.

## Scoring changes how teams work

Even with good leads, the order you call them in matters. A score turns a list into a queue.

Our bespoke lead generation tool for Starfish Debt Consultants, a sales call centre, helped cut lead qualification processing time by 50%. Qualifying faster means your best people spend their time on the leads most likely to convert.

## Building your own consented lead flow

- **Earn the interaction.** Give people a reason to engage: useful information, a quick quote or a fair competition.
- **Ask for consent plainly.** Say who will contact them, about what and how. Keep a record.
- **Capture signal, not just contact details.** A few well-chosen questions tell your team far more than a name.
- **Score and route automatically.** Warm leads should reach a person fast. Others can get a follow-up sequence.
- **Close the loop.** Feed outcomes back so scoring improves over time.

## The real cost per lead

A bought lead looks cheap until you count the calls, the time and the risk. A consented, scored lead costs effort to set up, but every one is a person who chose to hear from you.
