Most of the AI tools a business can sign up for today work the same way. Your data goes out to someone else's systems, gets processed, and an answer comes back. For a lot of tasks that's fine.

For some businesses it isn't. If you handle customer financial details, health information, or simply data you've promised to look after carefully, you want to know exactly where it goes and who can see it.

That's what private AI agents are for.

## What "private" means here

A private AI agent does the same jobs as any other agent: answering, qualifying, routing and reporting. The difference is where it runs and who controls it.

- **Hosted on your infrastructure.** The agent and its model run on systems you control, not a shared public service.
- **Data kept in South Africa.** Your customer and business data stays in the country.
- **Your agents, your rules.** You decide what the agent can access, what it can do on its own and what needs a person to approve.

Where it makes sense, we build and host a custom LLM for the client, so the model itself sits inside that boundary too.

## Who should care

**Financial services and insurance.** Customer data is sensitive, and the people responsible for it need clear answers about where it lives.

**Businesses with strict client contracts.** If your clients ask where their data is processed, "somewhere offshore" is not a comfortable answer.

**Anyone building AI into core operations.** Once an agent touches leads, quotes, payments or customer records, control matters more than convenience.

If your AI use is limited to drafting emails, you may not need this. If AI is becoming part of how the business runs, it's worth thinking about early.

## POPIA, plainly

POPIA places obligations on businesses that process personal information, including how it's secured and how it's shared. Responsibility for meeting those obligations stays with your business. No vendor can take it off your hands.

What the right setup can do is make those obligations easier to meet. Our private AI agents are built to support your POPIA obligations: hosting on your own infrastructure, data kept in South Africa, and logs of what the agents did, available on request.

## What to ask before you build

1. **Where will the model run, and where will our data be stored?** You should get a specific answer, not a general one.
2. **What can each agent see?** Give access to the data a task needs, and nothing more.
3. **What does the agent do alone, and what waits for a person?** This should be decided per task, not once for the whole system.
4. **Can we see what it did?** Logs of agent actions should be available when you ask for them.
5. **Who runs it day to day?** Private doesn't have to mean you manage it alone.
